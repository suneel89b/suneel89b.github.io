From Fictional Concepts to Changing How the World Works: A Timeline of AI

A team presentation that traces artificial intelligence from early ideas and fiction to today's generative AI, and reflects on how AI is changing people and the planet.

Team: Suneel Bandarupalli, Mounika Karri, Jagadeesh Kumar Kommineni, Joowon Lee, Sandra Luwedde, Pruthviraj Surendrasinh Solanki, and Asim Minhaj Syed. My part: the Generative AI slide (slide 6).

Description

The presentation moves through AI history in stages:

  • Early concepts: the Turing Test (1950), ELIZA (1966), and HAL 9000 (1968)
  • The math behind machine learning: from Gauss's regression to Arthur Samuel's checkers program
  • Early real-world AI: Deep Blue, AIBO, Roomba, and NASA's Mars rovers
  • The deep learning breakthrough: AlexNet
  • Generative AI: the rise of content-creating AI after the 2017 Transformer

It ends with a reflection on AI's positive and negative impacts on society and its environmental cost.

Objective

We created this timeline to understand how AI developed from an idea into a technology that shapes daily life, and to think critically about both its benefits and its costs.

Process

Our team of seven divided the AI timeline into eras, and each member researched and built one or more slides. I created the Generative AI slide. I researched how AI shifted from recognizing patterns to creating new content, and explained that the Transformer architecture and its self-attention mechanism, introduced in 2017, made this possible. I then showed how DALL·E (2021) and ChatGPT (2022) brought generative AI to the public and moved it from research into everyday workflows.

I kept the slide to three short points so a non-technical audience could follow it. Our team then combined all the slides into one deck with a consistent visual style.

Tools & Technologies

  • Microsoft PowerPoint (slide design)
  • ChatGPT (generating slide backgrounds)
  • Claude (research support)
  • GitHub Pages (publishing this portfolio)

Value Proposition

This presentation gives professionals a quick, visual overview of how AI evolved, helping them understand today's AI tools in context instead of seeing them as sudden or magical.

Unique Value

It does more than list dates. It connects AI history to real impacts on healthcare, education, work, privacy, and the environment, which shows the balanced view needed to adopt AI responsibly.

Relevance

For IT teams bringing AI into their operations, knowing how the technology developed, and what it costs in energy, privacy, and bias risk, helps them set realistic expectations and make responsible decisions.
