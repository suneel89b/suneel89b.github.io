About Me

From enterprise systems to satellite data to AI

I am a software developer with over 14 years of IT experience. I started my career as a JD Edwards CNC consultant after completing my bachelor's degree in electronics and computers. There I learned how large enterprise systems are installed, upgraded, and kept running.

After a few years in the industry, I earned a master's degree in computer science and worked as a graduate research assistant. In my research, I used a hybrid wavelet-artificial intelligence fusion approach (WAIFA) to create large data samples that improved the accuracy and performance of the Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC) algorithm. This was my first hands-on work applying AI to real data problems.

After graduation, I joined a data center as a front-end developer and worked on satellite imagery distribution platforms. I work closely with metadata quality, system upgrades, and download metrics. When an upgrade from Oracle Linux 7 to Oracle Linux 8 broke compatibility for our applications, we moved them from virtual machines to containers, one at a time, starting with the lowest risk.

Today, I am pursuing a master's degree in AI – Data Analytics at Indiana Wesleyan University. My goal is to bring AI into IT operations in a practical, reliable way, using it to catch data problems earlier, reduce downtime, and help teams make better decisions.
