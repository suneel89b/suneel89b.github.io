Professional Bio

From keeping systems running to making them smarter

I am an IT professional and a graduate student at Indiana Wesleyan University. In my work, I support applications that manage satellite imagery collections. Every day I deal with metadata quality, system upgrades, and download metrics, so I know how much reliable systems depend on clean, trusted data.

When an upgrade from Oracle Linux 7 to Oracle Linux 8 caused compatibility issues with our applications, I helped move them from virtual machines to containers. We moved them one by one, starting with the lowest risk, and tested each before it went live. That project taught me that new technology only works when it is planned carefully and when the people using it understand it.

My goal is to bring AI and machine learning into IT operations in a practical, reliable way, using it to catch data problems earlier, reduce downtime, and help teams make better decisions.
